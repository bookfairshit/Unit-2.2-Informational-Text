What students are doing

  • Blueprint Checkpoint (everyone, first): two excerpts from King’s speech, each traced through Words, Language, Appeal, Purpose and Effect, plus one blueprint sentence (page 6).
  • Stations A–C (groups): Foundation, Frame, Materials + Effect (pages 3–5). Only students pulled to the teacher table yesterday should still have one to finish.
  • Passport: a table glued in the spiral. The group’s Checker initials finished lines.

Collecting work

Collect the Blueprint Checkpoint. Teacher picks one before Thursday: (a) students photograph the checkpoint page and submit it on Canvas, or (b) spiral notebooks go in the class bin.

Students who were absent Tuesday or Wednesday

Students who missed Tuesday or Wednesday: start the Blueprint Checkpoint with page 1 (the Text Blueprint) beside them, then Station A.

If something goes wrong

  • No projector: write “1. Blueprint Checkpoint (quiet) 2. Finish stations 3. Passports” on the board; students use the printed pages.
  • A group is off task: ask the Checker to show you everyone’s passport.
  • A student is upset by the content: let them work quietly and leave a note for the teacher.
