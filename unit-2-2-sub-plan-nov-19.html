Before class

  • Open the deck to slide 15 (“Thursday”). Leave it up all period.
  • Students sit with their station groups (seating chart: teacher fills in where it is).
  • Transcripts of “I Have a Dream”: teacher fills in where they are (printed copies or the Canvas link).
  • Darter Huddle may shorten class today. The Checkpoint Desk comes first so it gets done either way.

Running the period

First 5 minAttendance. Then read aloud: “Today you finish the Dream Stations. First, 25 minutes of quiet, individual work at the Checkpoint Desk. Then you finish any station you haven’t done, with your group.”
Next 25 minCheckpoint Desk, quiet and individual (slide 12 shows the layout). Students write two boxes in their spiral: Excerpt A (the promise) and Excerpt B (the dream), each with Evidence, Purpose and Effect, plus a Mood line for B. Circulate; you do not need to teach the speech. If a student is stuck, point them to slide 11 and the sentence frame.
Rest of periodGroups finish any of Stations 1–3 they have not completed (slides 7–10). Groups who are done choose a bonus station (slide 13).
Last 3 minCheckers initial passports. Then collect the work (see below).

Read aloud at the start: “Today you finish the Dream Stations. First, 25 minutes of quiet, individual work at the Checkpoint Desk. Then you finish any station you haven’t done, with your group.”

What students are doing

  • Checkpoint Desk (everyone, first): two boxes in the spiral notebook. Excerpt A is the promise and Excerpt B is the dream, each with Evidence, Purpose and Effect, plus a Mood line for B.
  • Stations 1–3 (groups): Mood Detectives, Image Bingo, Evidence Ranking. Directions are on slides 7–10.
  • Passport: a table at the start of today’s spiral entry. Each group’s Checker initials finished lines.
  • Group jobs: Facilitator, Timekeeper, Checker, Materials + Tech. If a group drifts, ask the Checker to show you everyone’s passport.

Collecting work

Collect the Checkpoint Desk work. Teacher picks one before Thursday: (a) students photograph their Checkpoint Desk page and submit it on Canvas, or (b) spiral notebooks go in the class bin.

Students who were absent Tuesday or Wednesday

Students who missed Tuesday or Wednesday: do the Checkpoint Desk with the transcript, then Station 1 (Mood Detectives). They do not need to catch up on everything today.

If something goes wrong

  • No projector: write the four station names and “Checkpoint Desk first” on the board; groups use the slides on their devices.
  • A group is off task: ask the Checker to show you everyone’s passport line for the station they are on.
  • A student refuses or is upset by the content: let them work on the Checkpoint Desk quietly and leave a note for the teacher.
