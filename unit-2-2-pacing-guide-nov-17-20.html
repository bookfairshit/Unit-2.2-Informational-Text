The Text Blueprint: minute by minute

The one idea

Students leave understanding that an author’s purpose (PIE), rhetorical appeals and language choices are not separate skills. They work together to build the text. Every segment below serves that idea; when time runs short, keep what proves it.

PROTECT must happen   FLEX shrink if needed   CUT FIRST drop when behind

Period lengths assumed: Tue 45 · Wed 40 · Thu 40 · Fri 45 minutes. Bell schedule not verified; Thursday’s Darter Huddle may shorten class.

The arc

  1. Tue (You): Whole group: teach the blueprint (I do → We do → You do)
  2. Wed (You): Station discovery + explanation, with the teacher table pulling small groups
  3. Thu (Sub): Independent: Blueprint Checkpoint, then finish stations
  4. Fri (You): Small-group focus, then transfer to a new text (RFK)

Teacher-table plan

  • Tue night: sort exit sentences into label only · partial · connected (5 minutes).
  • Wed: round 1 = label only, round 2 = partial, round 3 = open. Pull 4–6 individual students per round.
  • Fri: re-sort from Thursday’s checkpoints; two groups, about 8 minutes each.
  • Group by observed need, not assumed level.

Print list (per period unless noted)

  • Pages 3–5 (stations): 3 copies each, in sheet protectors; reusable all day.
  • Page 1 (the blueprint): 1 per group, or project slide 3.
  • Page 2 (jobs + passport): 1 per student (cut the passport half).
  • Page 6 (checkpoint): 1 per student, or 1 per group if students write in the spiral.
  • Page 7 (Friday transfer): 1 per student not at the table.
  • Minimum run if printing is tight: pages 3–5 only; project the rest.
